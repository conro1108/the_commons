The Commons — privacy

The Commons is a small game made for friends. If you sign in with Google, it receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and uses them only to keep you signed in to the same account.

What you choose in the game — your name on file, your look, your filings and what you build — is shown to other players. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

To have your account and everything in it deleted, email conro1108@gmail.com.
